    How to play

    Guess the Pokémon card from its artwork. Tap the tiles to reveal what's underneath, but be careful: every tile you open costs points!

    When you're ready, type the card's exact name, including prefixes like "Team Rocket's" (e.g. Team Rocket's Raticate), and pick it from the suggestions. You have 2 guesses per card.

    Normal mode: each card puts 1,000 points in play. Every tile you open costs the points printed on it, and the first tile is free. Each wrong guess costs 50 points. A right answer scores the points still in play.

    Hard mode: each card puts 200 points in play. You can only open the outer tiles (5 points each). Each wrong guess costs 30 points.

    Daily: one card a day, the same for everyone, with the same free tile. A new card arrives at midnight UTC. Challenge the other players: can you name it with more points than they did?

    Booster: like opening a pack, guess ten random cards in a row. Open as many boosters as you like.

    Both come in Normal and Hard mode. Sign in with Google to save your results on every device and enter the leaderboard.

    Badges: earn medals for Daily challenges, streaks and Booster scores, in Normal and Hard mode. A Daily challenge is complete when you name the card.
