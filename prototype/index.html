    How to play

    You only see the card's artwork. Tap tiles to reveal it, then type the card's exact English name, including prefixes like "Team Rocket's" (e.g. Team Rocket's Raticate). You have 2 guesses per card.

    Each card puts 1,000 points in play. Every tile you open costs the points printed on it, and the first tile is free. Each wrong guess costs 50 points. A right answer scores the points still in play. Once you reach 0 you can still open tiles and guess, just for no points.
