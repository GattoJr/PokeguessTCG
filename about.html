About PokeguessTCG

PokeguessTCG is a free browser game for Pokémon Trading Card Game fans: name the card from a small part of its artwork.

Why it exists

Collectors know cards by their artwork long before they read the name. PokeguessTCG turns that into a daily puzzle: a new card every day, the same for everyone, plus Booster runs of ten cards and a Hard mode for the experts.

Who makes it

PokeguessTCG is made by a single fan in Italy, in their free time. It has no publisher and no sponsor.

The cards

Card names, sets and images come from TCGdex, an open database of Pokémon TCG cards (MIT licence). The game uses regular cards from the Scarlet & Violet, Sword & Shield and Mega Evolution series.

How it works

The game runs in your browser. Scores, the Daily schedule and accounts are kept on servers in the European Union. Signing in is optional and uses Google. Read the Privacy Policy for the details.
