Frequently asked questions

Short answers about the Daily card, scoring, accounts, ads and cookies.

When does the Daily card change?

Every day at midnight UTC: 1 am in Italy in winter, 2 am in summer. Everyone gets the same card and the same free tile.

Why can I only send names from the suggestions?

So a typo never costs you a guess. Type a few letters and pick the card from the list; capitals and accents don’t matter.

Is the answer the Pokémon or the card name?

The card name in English, exactly as printed, for example a trainer’s Pokémon or a name with a suffix. The suggestions show every name you can send.

How does the streak work?

It counts the days in a row on which you named the Daily card, in Normal or Hard. A missed or abandoned Daily doesn’t count, and the Booster doesn’t either.

What is Hard mode?

Only the 34 tiles on the edge can be opened, so you see much less of the artwork. Each card is worth 200 points and a wrong guess costs 30.

Do I need an account?

No. Guests can play every mode and earn badges in their browser. Signing in with Google keeps your results on every device and puts your best Booster on the leaderboard.

Where can I see past Daily cards?

In the Daily archive, with how all players did and your own score.

Why are there ads?

Ads pay for the servers and keep the game free for everyone. They never cover the card, the tiles or the guess box, and a few spaces on the page are all there is.

How do I change my cookie choices?

Choose Privacy choices at the bottom of any page. You can accept or refuse personalised ads at any time; if you refuse, you still see ads, just not based on your interests. The Cookie Policy lists everything the site stores.

Can I play with an ad blocker?

Yes, the game works the same. If you enjoy it, allowing ads on PokeguessTCG is the simplest way to support it.

Is this an official Pokémon game?

No. PokeguessTCG is an unofficial fan site, not affiliated with Nintendo, Creatures, GAME FREAK or The Pokémon Company.

How do I delete my account?

Open your profile (your name at the top), choose Delete account at the bottom and confirm. Your username, scores, badges and leaderboard place are deleted straight away and for good. If you can't sign in any more, write to pokeguesstcg@gmail.com from the email address of your account.

Something else? Write to pokeguesstcg@gmail.com.
