Cookie Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Cookies and similar storage are small pieces of data kept by your browser. PokeguessTCG uses some to make the game work, and Google uses some to show and, if you agree, personalise ads. You can change your choice at any time from .

Needed for the game

These items are kept in your browser's local storage and are needed for the game to work, so they don't require consent. They stay on your device: the game server only receives the guest ID and your games.

Ads by Google

Ads are served by Google AdSense. With your consent, Google and its partners use cookies to personalise ads and measure them. Without it, the ads are not personalised, but Google may still use cookies to limit how often you see an ad, for aggregated reporting and to prevent fraud. Names and durations are set by Google and may change; the main ones are:

Statistics

If we count visits, we only use cookie-free tools such as Cloudflare Web Analytics, which store nothing on your device and don't identify you.

Your choice

  • Accept all: personalised ads.
  • Reject all: ads that are not personalised. The game works exactly the same.
  • Customise: turn personalised ads on or off.

We keep your choice for 6 months, then ask again. You can also delete cookies and site data from your browser settings: the game then forgets your guest games on that device.
