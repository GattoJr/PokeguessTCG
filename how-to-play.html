How to play

A Pokémon card is hidden under 84 tiles. Open as few tiles as you can, then name the card. The fewer points you spend, the higher your score.

Tiles and points

Each card starts with 1,000 points in play. Opening a tile shows that part of the artwork and costs its value: the closer to the centre, the more it costs. The first tile (yellow here) is open for free.

Edge: 5 pointsInner ring: 15 pointsCentre: 60 pointsFree first tile

When the points reach 0 you can still open tiles and guess: the card just scores nothing.

Naming the card

  • Type the card’s English name and pick it from the suggestions. Only names from the list can be sent, so a typo never costs you a guess. Capitals and accents don’t matter.
  • You have 2 guesses per card. A wrong guess costs 50 points.
  • Stuck? Give up shows the card and scores 0.

Four ways to play

DailyOne card a day, the same for everyone, with the same free tile. It changes at midnight UTC.
BoosterTen random cards in a row, never today’s Daily. Up to 10,000 points.
HardOnly the 34 edge tiles can be opened. 200 points per card, a wrong guess costs 30.

Daily (Hard) has its own card, and Booster (Hard) its own leaderboard.

Streaks, badges and the leaderboard

  • Streak: the days in a row on which you named the Daily card, in Normal or Hard. Miss a whole day and it starts again.
  • Badges: 16 for each mode, for Daily cards named, streaks of 7, 14 and 30 days, and Booster scores.
  • Leaderboard: the best Booster of every player with an account, in Normal and Hard.

Playing without an account

You can play everything as a guest: your games are kept in this browser. Sign in with Google to keep them on every device and to appear on the leaderboard; the games you played as a guest move to your account.
