Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

PokeguessTCG is a free browser game: you name a Pokémon card from its artwork. This page explains what data the game uses, why, and what you can do about it. In short: you can play without an account; if you sign in, we keep your email address, your username and your game results, and nothing else. We don't sell data and we don't show ads.

Who is responsible

PokeguessTCG is run by a private individual based in Italy, who is the data controller for the data described here. Contact: pokeguesstcg@gmail.com.

Playing without an account

You can play every mode without signing in. To remember your games, your browser creates a random guest ID (a code that does not identify you) and stores it on your device together with the tiles you opened. The game server stores the guest ID with your games: the cards you played, the tiles you opened, your guesses and your score. These results also count, without any name, in the "how did other players do" charts.

If you sign in with Google

When you choose "Sign in with Google", Google tells us:

  • your email address and Google account ID, which identify your account;
  • your name and profile picture as stored in your Google account. Our sign-in service saves them with your account, but the game does not show or use them.

We never receive your Google password. We then store:

  • the username you choose, which other players see on the leaderboard;
  • your games and scores (the same data as for guests), including the games played as a guest in the same browser before signing in, which move to your account;
  • the sign-in session on your device, so you stay signed in.

The leaderboard publicly shows your username and your best Booster score. Your email address is never shown to other players.

Why we use this data

  • To run the game and your account: keeping scores, the leaderboard and your results across devices. Legal basis: providing the service you asked for (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep the game fair and working: for example spotting abuse or fixing errors. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a working, fair game (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)).

We don't use your data for advertising, profiling or marketing emails, and we don't sell it or share it with anyone except the service providers below.

Service providers

The game relies on these services, which process data on our behalf or receive the technical data (such as your IP address) that any website visit sends:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and image storage. Our data is stored in the EU (Ireland).
  • Google: "Sign in with Google" and the fonts used by the site (Google Fonts).
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.
  • jsDelivr: delivers a software library used by the site.
  • TCGdex: card images are loaded from TCGdex's servers.

Some of these companies may process technical data outside the European Union. When they do, they rely on the safeguards required by the GDPR, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Cookies and storage on your device

The game does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and it does not use analytics. It stores a few things in your browser's local storage so that the game works: the guest ID, the tiles you opened, the Booster in progress and, if you sign in, your session. If you sign in with Google, Google may set its own cookies to run the sign-in. If you delete your browser data, the game forgets your guest games on that device.

How long we keep it

Account data and games are kept while your account exists. Guest games stay on the server so that the daily statistics stay correct, but they are not linked to any personal information. You can delete your account yourself at any time from your profile (Delete account): your email address, your username and the games linked to the account are deleted straight away. If you can't sign in, write to us and we will delete it within 30 days.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, receive a copy of it, restrict how we use it, or object to its use. Write to pokeguesstcg@gmail.com from the email address of your account. You can also complain to the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or to the authority of the EU country where you live.

Children

The game itself needs no personal data. To create an account you must be at least 14 years old (the age of digital consent in Italy), or have a parent's permission.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. For important changes we will also tell signed-in players in the game.
