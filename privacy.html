PokeguessTCG Play

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

PokeguessTCG is a free browser game: you name a Pokémon card from its artwork. The game is paid for by ads served by Google. This page explains what data the game and the ads use, why, and what you can do about it. In short: you can play without an account; if you sign in, we keep your email address, your username and your game results; Google shows ads and, only if you agree, personalises them with cookies. We don't sell data.

Who is responsible

PokeguessTCG is run by a private individual based in Italy, who is the data controller for the data described here. Contact: pokeguesstcg@gmail.com.

Playing without an account

You can play every mode without signing in. To remember your games, your browser creates a random guest ID (a code that does not identify you) and stores it on your device together with the tiles you opened. The game server stores the guest ID with your games: the cards you played, the tiles you opened, your guesses and your score. These results also count, without any name, in the "how did other players do" charts.

If you sign in with Google

When you choose "Sign in with Google", Google tells us:

  • your email address and Google account ID, which identify your account;
  • your name and profile picture as stored in your Google account. Our sign-in service saves them with your account, but the game does not show or use them.

We never receive your Google password. We then store:

  • the username you choose, which other players see on the leaderboard;
  • your games and scores (the same data as for guests), including the games played as a guest in the same browser before signing in, which move to your account;
  • the sign-in session on your device, so you stay signed in.

The leaderboard publicly shows your username and your best Booster score. Your email address is never shown to other players.

Ads

Ads on PokeguessTCG are served by Google AdSense (Google Ireland Limited). To show an ad, Google receives technical data from your browser, such as your IP address, your browser and device, the page you are on and your general location, and it may read or set cookies and similar identifiers on your device.

  • Personalised ads, only if you agree: Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your interests and on your visits to this and other websites, and to measure how ads perform.
  • If you refuse: you still see ads, but they are not personalised. They are chosen from the page content and your general location. Google may still use cookies or similar technology to limit how often you see an ad, for aggregated reporting and to fight fraud and abuse.

You choose with the banner shown on your first visit, and you can change your choice at any time from at the bottom of every page. To learn more, read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and how Google uses cookies in advertising. You can also manage the ads Google shows you in My Ad Center. Google may pass data to its advertising partners; the list of cookies and storage is in the Cookie Policy.

Why we use this data

  • To run the game and your account: keeping scores, the leaderboard and your results across devices. Legal basis: providing the service you asked for (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To show personalised ads: only with your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time from Privacy choices.
  • To show non-personalised ads, which pay for the free game, and to keep the game fair and working, for example spotting abuse or fixing errors. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a working, fair and free game (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To count visits, if we use statistics: we would only use cookie-free tools, such as Cloudflare Web Analytics, that don't identify you.

We don't send marketing emails and we don't sell your data. Your games and account are never shared with advertisers.

Service providers

The game relies on these services, which process data on our behalf or receive the technical data (such as your IP address) that any website visit sends:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and image storage. Our data is stored in the EU (Ireland).
  • Google: ads (Google AdSense), "Sign in with Google" and the fonts used by the site (Google Fonts).
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.
  • jsDelivr: delivers a software library used by the site.
  • TCGdex: card images are loaded from TCGdex's servers.
  • Cloudflare: cookie-free visit statistics, if enabled.

Some of these companies may process technical data outside the European Union. When they do, they rely on the safeguards required by the GDPR, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Cookies and storage on your device

The game stores a few things in your browser so that it works: the guest ID, the tiles you opened, the Booster in progress, your settings, your cookie choice and, if you sign in, your session. These are needed for the game and don't require consent. Google's advertising cookies are described above. Every item, with its purpose and duration, is listed in the Cookie Policy.

How long we keep it

Account data and games are kept while your account exists. Guest games stay on the server so that the daily statistics stay correct, but they are not linked to any personal information. Your cookie choice is kept for 6 months, then we ask again. You can delete your account yourself at any time from your profile (Delete account): your email address, your username and the games linked to the account are deleted straight away. If you can't sign in, write to us and we will delete it within 30 days. Data processed by Google for ads is kept according to Google's own policies.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, receive a copy of it, restrict how we use it, or object to its use, and you can withdraw your consent to personalised ads at any time from Privacy choices. Write to pokeguesstcg@gmail.com from the email address of your account. You can also complain to the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or to the authority of the EU country where you live.

Children

The game itself needs no personal data. To create an account you must be at least 14 years old (the age of digital consent in Italy), or have a parent's permission. Children under 14 should not accept personalised ads without a parent's permission.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. For important changes we will also tell signed-in players in the game, and if they affect ads we will ask for your choice again.
